# Olist Delivery Performance & Customer Satisfaction Analytics

**Status:** In progress

## Project Brief

**Problem:** Olist customers sometimes give low reviews, and Olist doesn't know whether late delivery is one of the reasons.

**Who uses it:** The logistics team, who could decide which states to fix first, which sellers to chase for slow handovers, and what delivery date to promise customers.

**"Late" means:** The date the customer received the order is after the date Olist promised.

## Questions

1. Do late orders get lower review scores than on-time orders?
2. How much lower is the average score?
3. Which states have the most late orders?
4. How much sales money is tied to late orders?

## Hypotheses (my guesses, to be tested)

- Orders from sellers far from the customer arrive later.
- When a seller hands the package to the carrier late, the order arrives late.

## Data

Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset (Kaggle). The license is stated on the Kaggle page.
